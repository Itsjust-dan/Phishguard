# PhishGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

PhishGuard is a browser extension that checks websites for signs of phishing.

## What data is collected

When you open a web page (http or https), the extension sends that page's
address (URL) to the PhishGuard server so it can be checked. This includes the
path and query parts of the address. The part after "#" is removed before
sending. The extension does not read the contents of pages you visit, your
passwords, form entries, cookies or browsing history.

## How the URL is used

1. The PhishGuard server analyses the URL and downloads the public page at
   that address to inspect it for phishing signs.
2. If a site looks suspicious, the URL is also sent to VirusTotal
   (virustotal.com), a third-party security service, to check it against
   security engines. VirusTotal may keep and share URLs submitted to it, under
   its own privacy policy.
3. The result (safe, suspicious or phishing) is sent back to your browser.

## Storage

PhishGuard does not create accounts and does not keep a database of the URLs
you visit. The server keeps recent results in temporary memory only, to speed
up repeat checks. This is cleared whenever the server restarts. The server is
hosted on Render, which keeps standard technical logs (such as request times
and IP addresses) under its own privacy policy. The extension stores nothing
permanently on your device.

## What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it to
build profiles about you. Data is shared only with the services named above, and
only to perform the check.
